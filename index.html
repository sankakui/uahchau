I'm not responsible for delays caused by carriers, customs processing, or local delivery services once a package has left the origin country. Any customs duties charged in your country are the buyer's responsibility.
Items are purchased brand new and inspected before reshipping where possible. Minor shipping wear on outer packaging (not the item itself) is not grounds for a refund.
Buyers are responsible for providing an accurate shipping address and any version/member preferences at the time of order. Orders cannot be corrected after they've been placed with the retailer.
